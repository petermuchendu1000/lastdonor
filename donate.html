Support the General Fund

General fund donations help LastDonor review fundraisers, keep campaign pages online, process payments, support donors, and publish impact updates. If you want to support a specific person or family, you can browse active campaigns instead.

LastDonor charges 0% platform fees on fundraisers. The general fund is how donors, grantmakers, sponsors, and operating supporters help cover the platform work behind those fundraisers.

We take general fund donations in KES, paid by M-Pesa. LastDonor does not sell or ship physical goods. See the Refund Policy and Donor FAQ before giving.

General Fund FAQ

What does the LastDonor general fund support?
Giving to the general fund pays our staff to check campaigns, answer donors, and keep the site running.
Can I donate directly to a specific fundraiser instead?
Yes. Pick someone to help. Every campaign has been checked by a person before going live.
Does LastDonor charge a platform fee on donations?
We take 0%. The company that moves the money charges a fee to do it, and we receive no part of that fee. We pay our own bills from separate donations and grants.
Can I cancel a monthly general fund donation?
Yes. Monthly donations can be cancelled before the next billing date from your account when available or by contacting support@lastdonor.org.
Are general fund donations refundable?
Donations are generally final after processing, but LastDonor reviews refund requests for duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, technical errors, and compliance issues.
Is anything shipped after I donate?
No. LastDonor accepts donations only. There are no physical goods, shipping charges, delivery dates, returns, or product warranties.
