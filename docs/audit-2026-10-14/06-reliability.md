## Sub-areas examined with no finding

- **Donation idempotency (the money path).** Correct and layered. Unique index on `donations.stripe_payment_id` (`src/db/schema.ts:385`) + `onConflictDoNothing` (`payment-service.ts:103`) + early `return null` on the duplicate (`:105`) means a replayed `payment_intent.succeeded` performs no tally increment and fires no side effects. The dual-path design (client `donations/confirm` at `src/app/api/v1/donations/confirm/route.ts:54-57` racing the webhook) is safe for the same reason, and `confirm` re-retrieves the PaymentIntent from Stripe rather than trusting the client (`:52`). The `charge.refunded` handler guards on `donation.refunded` (`payment-service.ts:460`) and clamps with `GREATEST(…, 0)` (`:466-469`). Stripe metadata as the DR substrate is intact.
- **Cron authentication.** `verifyCronAuth` (`src/lib/cron-auth.ts:11-20`) length-checks then `timingSafeEqual`s; all 10 routes call it as their first statement. No finding.
- **Rate-limit degradation when Upstash is down.** The in-memory fallback is real and reachable: `checkRateLimitAsync` catches the Upstash error and falls through to `checkInMemory` (`src/lib/rate-limit.ts:215-221`), which has bounded growth (50 000 entries, 60 s sweep, oldest-first eviction at `:18-32`). It is per-instance, so effective limits multiply by instance count during an outage — documented behaviour, fail-open by design, not a defect. Webhook paths are exempted before any Redis call (`:186`), so an Upstash outage cannot block Stripe deliveries.
- **`safeCronError`.** Reviewed specifically for error swallowing. It is a redaction helper for audit-log bodies and is paired with a full `logError` at all three call sites. Not a swallowing bug. (The swallowing is in the bare `console.error` catches — R-11.)
- **AI malformed-output handling.** `call-ai.ts:202-242` is genuinely defensive: strips code fences, finds the first `[`/`{`, then walks the string with string/escape awareness to find the matching close bracket before `JSON.parse`, and throws a labelled error on failure (`:241`). Truncation is detected via `finish_reason === 'length'` and the response discarded in favour of the next model (`:121-127`); reasoning-token exhaustion is detected and retried with a 4× budget capped at 32 768 (`:109-119`). Provider outage and fallback-key exhaustion both terminate — the attempt list is finite and the loop cannot spin (`:76`), so there is no infinite retry. The gap is timeouts (R-06) and cost (R-07), not parsing.
- **`blog-generate` stuck-work recovery.** `blog-pipeline.ts:92-164` recovers topics stuck in `generating` past 30 minutes, caps at 3 attempts, and permanently rejects exhausted topics with a reason — the only cron in the repo with deliberate partial-failure recovery. Noted as an existing mitigation.
- **`reconcile` convergence.** Steps 1, 4 and 5 set columns to a derived value rather than incrementing, so re-firing after a partial failure is safe and a 300 s kill leaves only un-run later steps, not corruption. Its step-4 integrity sweep is what bounds R-12's blast radius.
- **Non-AI outbound fetches.** Every one found carries an explicit timeout (list in R-06 evidence). No bare-fetch finding.
