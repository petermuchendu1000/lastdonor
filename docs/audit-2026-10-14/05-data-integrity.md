## Sub-areas examined with NO findings

- **Columns in migrations but absent from `schema.ts` (dead/unmapped):** **none.** `diff.cjs` reports `only_in_migrations=0` across all 30 tables. `0030_remove_milestone_system.sql` correctly drops `campaigns.milestone_fund_release` plus `fund_releases`, `milestone_evidence` and `campaign_milestones`, and `schema.ts` no longer declares any of them; the two enums it drops (`fund_release_status`, `milestone_status`) are likewise absent from `schema.ts`.
