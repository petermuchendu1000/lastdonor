Where the money goes · 4 of 4

Read this before you agree

You will not get this money

Everything people give goes to the hospital, to the bill number you typed. It will not reach your own account at any point.

You cannot change it once we have sent money

You can change the hospital or the bill number up until the first payment leaves. After that it stays.

We still need to see your ID

Sending to a hospital does not replace the ID check. We still check who you are before any money moves.

Why the ID check stays Verification gates money out of the platform. Letting a destination substitute for it would let anyone raise, name a real hospital and a bill that is not theirs, and pay off a stranger's debt with other people's donations.
